Six months of AI-generated buy signals on my own portfolio. Up 13.8%.

That number is the least interesting thing in the write-up, for four
reasons — it's return on capital deployed rather than time-weighted, the
market was up too, N=1 with no control, and six months is noise.

Here's the part that actually taught me something.

44% of the gain came from one position that was never in my target
allocation. It surfaced on the watch list, came back a STRONG BUY at 91%
confidence with an RSI of 9.3, and I bought it off-plan — exactly the
decision allocation targets exist to prevent.

Then I ignored the one instruction the tool gave me. It said set a limit at
$360. I paid $370.14 at market because I didn't want to miss it. The stock
traded to $349.20 two days later.

That cost me about four percentage points. I built the limit-price feature,
I read its reasoning, I agreed with it, and I overrode it anyway.

The tool was right and the human was in a hurry. If you build something like
this for yourself, budget for that failure mode — it isn't the model, it's
you at 11pm deciding you know better.
